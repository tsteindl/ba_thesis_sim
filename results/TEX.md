\caption{Budget improvement over the brute-force baseline at $90\%$ convergence, and in parentheses the share of the \emph{attainable} improvement it realises, $(\rho-1)/(\rho_{\mathrm{ceil}}-1)$. The ceiling $\rho_{\mathrm{ceil}}$ grants perfect knowledge of the optimal depth $N=\lfloor\pi/2\phi\rfloor$ but still requires the estimate to be resolved by the sampling law of Eq.~(3.4), giving $\Pr(\text{converge})=2\Phi(2\epsilon\sqrt{NC})-1$.
